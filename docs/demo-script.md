# Mentora Demo Video Script

**Length:** about 8 minutes (about 1,000 spoken words)
**Accounts:** `student1@mentora.test` / `Student123!` and `instructor1@mentora.test` / `Instructor123!`
**Setup:** run `php spark db:seed DatabaseSeeder` first so there is demo data. Start the question service if you want to show AI questions.

**Skip in the video** (not built yet): Register, Forgot Password, My Profile, Settings, Help & Support, Notifications, Dark Mode, and the Grades, Announcements and Inbox tiles in the Apps menu. Parent and Chairperson accounts have no pages yet.

Each scene gives what to show, then what to say.

---

## 1. Intro (0:00 – 0:20)

**Show:** Login page.

> "This is Mentora, a learning management system. Let's take a quick tour."

## 2. Login (0:20 – 0:45)

**Show:** Type the student email and password. Click the eye icon. Tick "Remember me". Click Login.

> "Log in with your email and password. The eye icon shows your password. Remember me keeps you signed in."

## 3. Student Dashboard (0:45 – 1:30)

**Show:** Scroll through the dashboard.

> "This is the student dashboard. You see your profile and key numbers.
> Upcoming tasks show what is due.
> My Courses lists your classes.
> Announcements and reminders keep you updated.
> Quick Access links to other pages."

**Show:** Open the Apps menu (grid icon). Point out Courses, Calendar, Schedule.

> "The Apps menu works from any page."

## 4. Courses and Overview (1:30 – 2:15)

**Show:** Click Courses. Open one course (for example, Computer Programming 1). Stay on Overview.

> "Here are all your courses. Open one.
> Overview shows your progress, deadlines, recent activity, and the next lesson to continue."

## 5. Announcements (2:15 – 2:40)

**Show:** Click the Announcements tab. Open a post. Add a reaction. Write a reply.

> "Announcements are posted by your instructor. You can react and reply."

## 6. Modules and Lessons (2:40 – 3:40)

**Show:** Click Modules. Open a module. Open a video lesson, then a PDF, then a reading. Click Complete on one. Show a locked item.

> "Modules hold your lessons.
> Lessons can be videos, PDFs, readings, links, or discussions.
> Mark a lesson complete to track progress.
> Some lessons stay locked until you finish the ones before."

## 7. Resources (3:40 – 3:55)

**Show:** Click Resources. Switch between grid and list view. Open a folder.

> "Resources stores course files in folders. View them as a grid or a list."

## 8. Discussions (3:55 – 4:25)

**Show:** Click Discussions. Open a thread. Post a reply with an attachment. React to a post.

> "Discussions are for questions and sharing ideas. Reply, attach files, and react."

## 9. Assessments (4:25 – 5:10)

**Show:** Click Assessments. Open a quiz. Start it. Answer a few questions. Submit. Show the score. Then open an assignment and submit work.

> "Assessments are quizzes and assignments.
> Take a quiz, then submit it. Your score shows right away for auto-graded questions.
> For assignments, upload your work and submit."

## 10. Grades, Calendar, Schedule (5:10 – 5:50)

**Show:** Click Grades tab. Then open Calendar from the Apps menu. Add a reminder. Mark it done. Then open Schedule.

> "Grades shows your scores and your standing in each course.
> The Calendar shows deadlines and events. Add your own reminders and mark them done.
> Schedule shows your weekly class timetable."

**Show:** Click the profile menu. Click Logout.

> "That's the student side. Now the instructor."

## 11. Instructor Login and Dashboard (5:50 – 6:15)

**Show:** Log in as `instructor1@mentora.test`. Show the dashboard.

> "Instructors get their own dashboard.
> It shows teaching load, the grading queue, and students at risk."

## 12. Build Content (6:15 – 7:00)

**Show:** Open a course. Go to Modules. Create a module. Add a learning unit. Add a lesson. Upload a video or PDF. Set a prerequisite. Reorder two items.

> "Instructors build the course here.
> Create a module, add a unit, then add lessons.
> Upload videos and PDFs.
> Set prerequisites to control the order.
> Drag to reorder."

## 13. Announcements and Discussions (7:00 – 7:20)

**Show:** Post an announcement. Pin it. Go to Discussions. Pin a thread, lock a thread, and mark an answer.

> "Post and pin announcements.
> In discussions, pin or lock threads and mark the best answer."

## 14. Create a Quiz (7:20 – 8:10)

**Show:** Open Assessments. Create a quiz. Open the quiz builder. Add a question. Show a rubric on an assignment. Click AI Generate and review the questions. Accept one. Show Import and Export.

> "Create a quiz in the quiz builder. Add questions by hand.
> Or use AI to draft questions. Review them, then accept or discard.
> You can also import and export questions.
> Assignments can use rubrics for grading."

## 15. Grade Work and Gradebook (8:10 – 9:00)

**Show:** Open an assignment. Click Grade Work. Pick a student. Score by rubric. Add feedback. Submit the grade. Then open Grades. Show the scheme editor (categories, weights, periods). Show the gradebook. Enter a manual score. Override a final grade. Click Export.

> "Grade student work one by one. Score with the rubric and add feedback.
> In Grades, set up categories, weights, and grading periods.
> The gradebook calculates final grades.
> Enter manual scores, or override a final grade.
> Export or import grades with a spreadsheet."

## 16. Close (9:00 – 9:20)

**Show:** Return to the dashboard.

> "That's Mentora. Courses, lessons, quizzes, and grades, all in one place. Thanks for watching."

---

## Recording Tips

- Use a seeded course with videos, PDFs, and submitted work already in place.
- Keep the browser zoom at 100% and the window at 1080p.
- Pause one second after each click so viewers can follow.
- Cut any waiting time, such as AI generation or uploads.
